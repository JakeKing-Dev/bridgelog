BridgeLog

Accessibility

Updated October 2026

The goal. BridgeLog aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, so staff who use screen readers, larger text, keyboards or high contrast can log as quickly as anyone else.

What's built in

  • Light and dark modes that follow the device setting, with text contrast checked against WCAG AA in both.
  • Large tap targets for logging in the middle of a lesson.
  • Every button and form field has a text name for screen readers, and every image has a text description.
  • Pages reflow on a phone and work with browser zoom.
  • Lexend, a typeface designed for readability.
  • Timers can chime as well as show on screen.

What's been checked

In October 2026, the app, this site and the privacy fact sheet were checked automatically for text contrast in light and dark mode, unlabeled buttons and fields, images without descriptions and page language. Problems found were fixed, and all pages passed those checks.

Automated checks catch only part of what matters. BridgeLog hasn't yet had a full manual review or been tested by screen-reader users, so there may be gaps we don't know about.

Known gaps

  • The four-week calendar and pattern views are visual summaries. The same information is in the timeline and written summaries, which work with screen readers.

Report a problem

If something in BridgeLog is hard to use with your device, setup or assistive technology, please email and say what you were trying to do. Accessibility problems are a priority.
